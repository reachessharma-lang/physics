(b) Change in mass:

Because the glass rod lost electrons, its mass decreased.

\[\Delta m = n \times m_e = (3.0 \times 10^{13}) \times (9.1 \times 10^{-31} \text{ kg}) = 2.73 \times 10^{-17} \text{ kg}\]

(a) Force in air:

\[F_{\text{vac}} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{\vert{}q_1 q_2\vert{}}{r^2} = \frac{(9 \times 10^9) \times (2 \times 10^{-6}) \times (6 \times 10^{-6})}{(0.1)^2} = \frac{0.108}{0.01} = 10.8 \text{ N}\]

Nature: Attractive (since charges have opposite signs).

(b) Force in oil (\(\varepsilon_r = 5\)):

\[F_{\text{med}} = \frac{F_{\text{vac}}}{\varepsilon_r} = \frac{10.8 \text{ N}}{5} = 2.16 \text{ N}\]

(c) Equivalent distance \(r'\) in oil:

To experience force equal to \(F_{\text{vac}}\):

\[\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r} \frac{\vert{}q_1 q_2\vert{}}{(r')^2} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{\vert{}q_1 q_2\vert{}}{r^2} \implies (r')^2 = \frac{r^2}{\varepsilon_r}\] \[r' = \frac{r}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}} = \frac{10 \text{ cm}}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{10}{2.236} \approx 4.47 \text{ cm}\]

1. Force due to \(q_1 = +3 \ \mu\text{C}\) at vertex A:

Both \(q_1\) and \(q_0\) are positive, so repulsion force \(\vec{F}_1\) acts vertically downwards along \(\vec{AD}\):

\[F_1 = \frac{(9 \times 10^9) \times (3 \times 10^{-6}) \times (1 \times 10^{-6})}{(0.2598)^2} = \frac{0.027}{0.0675} = 0.40 \text{ N (downwards along AD)}\]

2. Force due to \(q_2 = +3 \ \mu\text{C}\) at vertex B:

Repulsive force \(\vec{F}_2\) acts along \(\vec{BD}\) (towards right, along \(BC\)):

\[F_2 = \frac{(9 \times 10^9) \times (3 \times 10^{-6}) \times (1 \times 10^{-6})}{(0.15)^2} = \frac{0.027}{0.0225} = 1.20 \text{ N (towards C)}\]

3. Force due to \(q_3 = -3 \ \mu\text{C}\) at vertex C:

Attractive force \(\vec{F}_3\) acts along \(\vec{DC}\) (towards right, along \(BC\)):

\[F_3 = \frac{(9 \times 10^9) \times (3 \times 10^{-6}) \times (1 \times 10^{-6})}{(0.15)^2} = 1.20 \text{ N (towards C)}\]

4. Resultant Force Calculation:

Total horizontal force component along base \(BC\) (towards right):

\[F_x = F_2 + F_3 = 1.20 \text{ N} + 1.20 \text{ N} = 2.40 \text{ N}\]

Total vertical force component along altitude \(AD\) (downwards):

\[F_y = F_1 = 0.40 \text{ N}\]

Magnitude of net force:

\[F_{\text{net}} = \sqrt{F_x^2 + F_y^2} = \sqrt{(2.40)^2 + (0.40)^2} = \sqrt{5.76 + 0.16} = \sqrt{5.92} \approx 2.43 \text{ N}\]

Direction angle \(\theta\) below the base line \(BC\):

\[\tan\theta = \frac{F_y}{F_x} = \frac{0.40}{2.40} = 0.1667 \implies \theta = \tan^{-1}(0.1667) \approx 9.46^\circ\]
